# Asset sources · Sen An Nhiên V3

The source code, scene construction, clothing, rig and animations are authored for this project. The following third-party assets are distributed locally; gameplay does not contact these services.

## Seated Buddha · CC0 1.0

- **10th C CE Thai Seated Buddha - black background**, Minneapolis Institute of Art.
- Original: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/10th-c-ce-thai-seated-buddha-black-background-31857e30bf9249f788aed2dfe5eb6673
- Museum context: https://new.artsmia.org/with-new-light-mias-reinstalled-himalayan-south-and-southeast-asian-art-galleries/the-arts-of-southeast-asia-distinctive-identities-in-a-cosmopolitan-world
- License listed by the original model API: **CC0 Public Domain**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Distributed file: `public/assets/models/seated-buddha.glb`; original textures are embedded. Scene changes: normalized size, material response and separate pedestal/halo.
- Downloaded through the Allen Institute for AI **Objaverse** mirror: https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse . Attribution for the Objaverse database: Deitke et al., *Objaverse: A Universe of Annotated 3D Objects*, https://objaverse.allenai.org/ . Objaverse database metadata is ODC-By 1.0; individual model rights remain the original CC0 license.

## Human head · MakeHuman mesh · CC0 1.0

- Derived from the MakeHuman Community **hm08 base mesh**, not the application's code.
- Mesh: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
- Asset license: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md
- CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Changes: cropped head, normalized size, vertex colors, smooth normals, converted to `human-head.glb`. Generated by `scripts/build-human-head.mjs`.
- Clothing, eyes, hands, procedural rig and animation are separate project geometry. Gender selection changes head/body proportions; both characters wear temple attire and have shaved heads.
