The market shopkeepers are derivatives of the existing Sen An Nhiên player model, `../thay-chua-chibi.glb`. The original remains unchanged; see the project's existing character asset credits.

The counter pose, linen apron, vertex-colour variants, static head/hair separation, LODs and Meshopt compression are generated by `scripts/prepare-market-vendors.ts`. Female hair follows the game's `src/FemaleChibiHair.tsx` design. The generation report records the source SHA-256 in `vendor-budget.json`.
